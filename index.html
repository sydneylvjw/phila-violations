Building Code Enforcement in Philadelphia

Sydney Jones, MUSA 6110

Philadelphia has some of the oldest housing stock in the country. The median age is 30 years above the nation.

Residential property safety is a significant concern for residents of Philadelphia. In addition to having some of the oldest housing stock in the nation with a median age around 30 years greater than the nation, Philadelphia is also among the most racially and economically segregated, with more than 20 percent of its residents living below the poverty line (U.S. Census, 2023).

Many properties around the city have been deemed unsafe due to property maintenance violations.

Some parts of the city, typically the areas with the highest poverty rates, have properties that have been determined to be an imminent danger to the neighborhood and public.

Where Do We Go From Here?

Although code enforcement can help reduce the number of vacant and nuisance properties, it does nothing to address the conditions that contribute to it:
Challenges accessing employment and municipal disinvestment that has led to property dispossession and urban infrastructure decline.
