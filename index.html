2: Housing Conditions

Philadelphia's housing stock is old, with a median age more than 30 years higher than the nation.

Thousands of properties around the city have been deemed unsafe due to property maintenance violations.

As the map shows, thousands of properties across Philadelphia have been deemed unsafe for inhabitation by the Department of Licenses and Inspections.

Many unsafe properties are clustered in parts of the city that have struggled to recouperate from decades of disinvestment.

These communities often have majority-Black or Latine populations, and sometimes have the highest poverty rates. Poverty and residential segregation are intimately tied to housing conditions.

5: Property Maintenance

Some parts of the city, typically the areas with the highest poverty rates, have properties that have been determined to be an imminent danger to the neighborhood and public.
