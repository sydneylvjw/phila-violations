Housing Conditions

The median age of Philadelphia's housing stock is more than 30 years older than the nation.

Residential Concerns

Property safety is a significant concern for residents of Philadelphia. In addition to having some of the oldest housing stock in the nation with a median age around 30 years greater than the nation, Philadelphia is also among the most racially and economically segregated, with more than 20 percent of its residents living below the poverty line (U.S. Census, 2023).

Property Maintenance

Many properties around the city have been deemed unsafe due to property maintenance violations.

Some parts of the city, typically the areas with the highest poverty rates, have properties that have been determined to be an imminent danger to the neighborhood and public.
